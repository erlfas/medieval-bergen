📖 Slik bygger du en Laftehytte (How to Build a Norwegian Cabin)

🌲 1. Felling Timber (10-Chop Spruce Physics)

Approach tall spruce trees on the Bergen hillsides and strike with your axe (Left-Click or E).

  • Spruce trees take 10 axe chops. Dynamic notch cuts carve into the trunk.
  • The tree tilts and crashes down using rigid body physics based on cut orientation.
  • Upon hitting the earth, branches shatter off and the trunk splits into carryable timber logs!

🪵 2. Hauling Timber & Carried Log Integration

Approach any fallen log and press E to pick it up and carry it in your settler's arms.

  • Carry logs directly to your building plot. Drop them anytime with E or G.
  • Direct Construction: When you hold a log in your arms and press B to build, that carried log is automatically consumed into the cabin foundation (-1 log cost)!

🪨 3. Harvesting Foundation Stones, Birch Bark & Turf

  • Slate & Field Stones (Grunnmur): Gather 4 stones from scree slopes to raise the bottom logs off damp soil and prevent rotting.
  • Birch Bark (Never): Harvest 4 white birch bark sheets for water-tight underlayment beneath the roof turf.
  • Mountain Meadow Sod (Torv): Cut 4 living green turf blocks to insulate against brutal Norwegian winters.

🔨 4. Build Mode, Architectural Ghost & Door Alignment

Press B to toggle Build Mode. You can build either the Complete Laftehytte or build stage-by-stage (Foundation → Walls → Roof → Hearth):

  • Architectural Ghost: See the transparent green silhouette of the cabin, including walls, roof pitch, and the open doorway!
  • Door Orientation: Press R to rotate 90°. Watch the entrance threshold step to face the doorway south or west away from prevailing mountain gales.
  • Place: Left-Click to place your cabin firmly on the ground.

🔥 5. Living in the Årestue (Warmth & Hearth)

  • 100% Shelter: Step inside! The enclosed walls and turf roof shield you completely from Bergen's rain, snow, and winter frostbite.
  • Central Hearth (Åre): The stone hearth emits 360° radiant warmth and crackling firelight. Smoke vents up cleanly through the open roof smoke hole (ljore).
  • Cooking: Press H near the hearth fire to roast venison and restore health!
