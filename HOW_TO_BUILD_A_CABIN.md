# Slik bygger du en Laftehytte / How to Build a Cabin (Bergen 1360)

In **Medieval Bergen 1360**, the rugged coastal fjords and mountains bring heavy rains, freezing North Sea blizzards, and roaming wolf packs. Building a traditional Norwegian log cabin (**Laftehytte / Årestue**) is the settler's highest priority for survival.

This guide details both the historical vernacular building techniques used in 14th-century western Norway and the gameplay mechanics to construct your shelter.

---

## Historical Architecture: The 1360 Bergen *Årestue*

A 14th-century Bergen dwelling (*årestue*) was designed to withstand extreme moisture and cold without mortar or metal nails:

1. **Grunnmur (Dry Fieldstone Foundation):**
   Stacked slate and granite boulders elevate the bottom sill timber (*svill*) off the wet fjord ground, preventing moisture decay and frost-heaving.
2. **Lafteverk (Interlocking Notched Timber Walls):**
   Slow-grown spruce and pine logs are debarked, notched at the corners (*nov* or *lafteknute*), and stacked horizontally. As the timber dries, its own weight tightens the joints into a wind-tight wall.
3. **Gavler og Mønsås (Triangular Log Gables & Ridge Beam):**
   Heavy triangular log gables support a massive central ridge beam (*mønsås*) running along the roof crest.
4. **Torvtak & Never (Birch Bark & Living Sod Roof):**
   Sheets of white birch bark (*never*) are laid in overlapping layers to create an impervious waterproof membrane. Heavy blocks of grassy meadow sod (*torv*) are placed on top to weigh down the bark and provide thick thermal insulation against freezing winter cold.
5. **Åre & Ljore (Central Stone Hearth & Smoke Hole):**
   A dry-stone open firepit (*åre*) sits in the center of the wooden floor, radiating 360° warmth. Smoke gently rises up through an open skylight / smoke hole (*ljore*) in the roof ridge.
6. **Langbenk / Bris (Interior Wall Sleeping Bench):**
   Built-in spruce timber benches line the perimeter walls, keeping settlers off the cool floor to rest, sleep on sheepskins, and stay close to the fire.

## Step-by-Step Gameplay Guide

### Step 1: Felling Spruce & Pine Trees (10-Chop Dynamic Notch System)
- Locate mature spruce trees on the hillsides and coastal slopes.
- Equip your axe and strike the tree using **[Left-Click]** or **[E]**.
- **Dynamic Physics:** Each chop carves a progressive undercut notch into the trunk. After **10 chops**, the tree tilts along its felling notch and crashes down under full rigid-body physics.
- **Log Splitting:** When the trunk strikes the ground, its branches shatter off and the main stem splits into carryable timber logs.

### Step 2: Hauling Logs (Physical Log Carrying System)
- Walk up to any fallen timber log and press **[E]** to lift it.
- Your settler will carry the heavy log in front of them with both arms.
- Press **[E]** or **[G]** to set the log down on your chosen building plot.
- **Carried Log Construction Bonus:** If you are holding a log in your arms when placing a building piece in Build Mode, that carried log is **automatically consumed directly** into the construction, deducting 1 log from the inventory cost!

### Step 3: Gathering Foundation Stones, Birch Bark & Meadow Turf
- **Field Stones (Grunnmur & Åre):** Gather 4 to 6 stones from the rocky scree slopes along the foot of Mount Fløyen.
- **Birch Bark (Never):** Harvest 4 sheets of white birch bark from nearby birch groves for water-tight roof underlayment.
- **Sod Turf (Torv):** Cut 4 blocks of green grassy meadow turf for thermal insulation.

### Step 4: Entering Build Mode & Aligning the Doorway
- Press **[B]** to toggle **Build Mode**.
- Use **[Tab]** or **[Q]** / Scroll wheel to cycle through recipes:
  - **Option A — Complete Laftehytte:** Assembles the entire finished cabin at once (6 logs, 4 stone, 4 bark, 4 turf).
  - **Option B — Stage-by-Stage Construction:**
    - *Stage 1 (Grunnmur):* Stone footings & heavy timber plank floor.
    - *Stage 2 (Veggløft):* 4 interlocking lafteverk timber walls with framed doorway.
    - *Stage 3 (Torvtak):* Triangular gables, ridge beam, and green sod roof.
    - *Stage 4 (Åre Ildsted):* Central stone hearth, embers, rising smoke, and sleeping bench.
- **Architectural Ghost Preview:** You will see a transparent emerald-green silhouette of the cabin, showing the roof pitch, walls, and the open door opening.
- **Rotating the Doorway:** Press **[R]** to rotate the cabin in 90° increments. Watch the entrance step indicator to align the doorway towards your preferred path (facing away from prevailing mountain winds).
- **Placing:** **[Left-Click]** to place your cabin.

### Step 5: Surviving Inside the Cabin
- **100% Shelter Protection:** Step inside through the open doorway. The `ShelterDetector` automatically registers overhead roof enclosure and protective timber walls, completely preventing hypothermia from coastal rain, blizzards, and wind chill.
- **The Åre Fireplace:** The central stone hearth crackles with glowing embers and warm firelight, with smoke drifting up through the roof ljore. Standing near the hearth warms your settler and enables food roasting (**[H]** to roast raw venison into cooked meat).
- **Wolf Defense:** Wolves cannot penetrate the solid log walls, providing a safe sanctuary through the long Bergen nights.
